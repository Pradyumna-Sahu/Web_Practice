About NIST University

NIST University is located at Institute Park, Berhampur, Odisha. The university provides undergraduate, postgraduate, and research opportunities across areas including engineering, science, management, and health sciences.

Its campus includes academic buildings, laboratories, classrooms, library facilities, student residences, and spaces for sports and student activities.

Our Mission

To support academic excellence, fundamental research, innovation, global citizenship, and collaborative engagement.

Our Vision

To encourage high-quality learning, creative innovation, entrepreneurship, and partnerships.
